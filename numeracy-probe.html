Step 2 — Before you begin

Administration protocol

One item at a time

Twelve items across five domains. There is no going back — a revisited item is no longer a first-response measure.

No feedback, ever

The probe never says right or wrong, and neither should you. Correction mid-administration contaminates every item after it.

Record the method, not just the answer

After each response, mark how it was reached. A correct answer reached by counting on fingers at age fourteen is the finding.

“I don't know” is data

Accept it without prompting and move on. Do not encourage guessing, and do not wait out a long silence — record it and continue.

Response latency runs automatically

Timing starts when the item appears and stops on submit. Read items aloud promptly if you are reading; a slow read inflates the latency.

Two items ask for written work

On the procedural items, transcribe the intermediate steps as given. Where the algorithm breaks is more informative than whether it broke.

Numeracy Diagnostic Probe · Form A · informal criterion-referenced probe — not a normed instrument and not a diagnosis.
Item construction draws on principles from the Feifer Assessment of Mathematics, WIAT-4 Numerical Operations and Math Problem Solving, WJ-IV Math Facts Fluency, the Butterworth Dyscalculia Screener, and the WISC-V Figure Weights paradigm. It reproduces no published item.
