How this works

Twelve questions, and no score at the end

You can't pass or fail this

Nothing here goes on a report card. It's working out which bits of maths come easily to you and which bits are fiddly, so you get help with the right things.

Some of it is meant to be too hard

The questions get harder on purpose until they're harder than you can do. That's how it's built, so getting stuck doesn't mean anything has gone wrong.

“I don't know” is a real answer

There's a button for it. Using it tells us more than a guess does, and nobody minds.

You won't be told right or wrong

Not until the very end. That's deliberate — knowing would change how you did the next one.

Answer first, then say how you did it

Type your answer and press Enter. Then you'll get a couple of questions about how you worked it out. Take as long as you like on those — they're not timed.

One at a time, and no going back

Once you've answered, that one is done. Somewhere quiet helps, and try not to switch to another tab partway through a question.

Practice — separate from assessment

Build the skill, don't rehearse the test

Untimed, unscored, and as many items as you want. Every item comes from the same generators the probe uses, so nothing here is a preview of a specific question — but the strategies are the ones the report recommends, and the walk-throughs teach the derivation rather than handing over an answer.

Keep practice and assessment apart. Practising the underlying skills between probes is the point — that is instruction working. Two domains are different: the balance scales and the word problems measure how you cope with an unfamiliar structure, so getting used to those formats can lower the load without the underlying capacity changing. Practice volume is logged per domain and shown alongside any comparison, so a gain can be read in that light. Leave a clear gap before re-probing, and never practise immediately beforehand.

History and comparison

Completed administrations are kept in this browser only. Select two to compare them — the report reads change in strategy, latency, and error type as well as accuracy, because with two or three items per domain accuracy alone moves far too easily to trust on its own.

Numeracy Diagnostic Probe · Form A · This is a practice-and-check tool, not a real test. It cannot diagnose anything, and nothing it says should be treated as a medical or educational decision on its own.
Question design draws on principles from the Feifer Assessment of Mathematics, WIAT-4 Numerical Operations and Math Problem Solving, WJ-IV Math Facts Fluency, the Butterworth Dyscalculia Screener, and the WISC-V Figure Weights paradigm. It reproduces no published item.
